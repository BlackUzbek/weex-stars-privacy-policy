# VexStars Privacy Policy
**Last updated: October 10, 2026**

VexStars (`@VexuStarsbot`) provides Telegram Stars, Telegram Gifts, balance management, and related services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our bot.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you use VexStars, we may collect and process:

- **Telegram account information:** Telegram user ID, username, and display name.
- **Transaction information:** balance, deposits, purchase amounts, order history, and transaction status.
- **Payment verification information:** payment receipts or screenshots voluntarily submitted by users.
- **Recipient information:** Telegram user ID or username provided to deliver a gift or other purchased service.
- **Messages and support requests:** information you send to the bot or its administrators.

Please do not send passwords, Telegram login codes, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Provide Telegram Stars and Gifts services.
- Process purchases, deposits, and refunds.
- Verify payment receipts.
- Maintain user balances and transaction records.
- Provide customer support and resolve disputes.
- Detect fraud, unauthorized transactions, and misuse.
- Maintain and improve the security and reliability of the service.

## 3. Payments and Financial Information

VexStars may request payment receipts or screenshots to verify bank transfers. Users should hide unrelated personal and financial information before submitting a receipt.

We do not ask users to provide their bank passwords, card PINs, or Telegram verification codes. Payment verification may be performed manually by an authorized administrator.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored in the database and hosting infrastructure used to operate VexStars. Access should be limited to authorized personnel who need it to operate the service.

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users’ personal information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to:
- Deliver a requested gift or service through Telegram or a service provider.
- Verify or resolve a payment or transaction issue.
- Protect the service against fraud or abuse.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Third-party services may process information under their own privacy policies.

## 6. Data Retention

Transaction records may be retained for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and service operation. Other information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes, subject to applicable legal requirements.

## 7. User Choices and Requests

You may contact our support team to request information about your stored data or ask for correction or deletion where appropriate.

Some transaction records may need to be retained to resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or comply with legal obligations.

## 8. Children

VexStars is not intended to encourage children to make unauthorized purchases. If you are not legally able to make a purchase independently, obtain the required permission from a parent or legal guardian.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or data practices change. The latest version should be published at the official policy link.
